Help & Tips

Search Mode: Type at least 2 characters to search matching text anywhere within artist or song names.

Filter Mode:

  • Type 1 character → shows only artists starting with that letter.
  • Type 2+ characters → shows artists or songs starting with that text.

Browse By:

  • Artists → Browse alphabetically by artist.
  • Songs → Browse alphabetically by song title.
  • My Favorites⭐ → Browse your favorites list alphabetically by song title.

Expand/Collapse: Use the Expand All / Collapse All buttons to quickly open or close all artists or song groups.

Requesting Songs: Tap the Request button beside any song to submit a performance request.

Favorites List: Tap the ⭐star beside any song to add it to your "My Favorites" view.

Search Scope: This is an additional menu that appears when on the My Favorites⭐ view that lets you select "where" you want to search/filter artists or songs:

  • Search within Favorites Only.
  • Search the Entire Library.
